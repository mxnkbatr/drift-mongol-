Privacy Policy – Drift King: Steppe Neon

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Drift King: Steppe Neon ("the game") is developed by Munkhbaatar Dorjsuren. This policy explains what data the game uses.

Data we collect

  • Guest player ID: a random identifier generated on your device so your leaderboard scores can be grouped. It is not linked to your real identity.
  • Display name: the nickname you choose when submitting a score. It is shown publicly on the leaderboard. Offensive names are filtered.
  • Scores and run statistics: your score, route, weather and run stats, stored on our leaderboard server when you submit a score.

Settings and personal bests are stored locally on your device.

What we do not do

  • No account or login is required.
  • No advertising and no ad SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no selling of data.
  • No location, contacts, photos or payment data is collected.

Retention and deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard runs. To request removal of your entries, email us with your display name.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the nickname and scores described above.
