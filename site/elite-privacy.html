Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy covers the Elite Travel Mongolia website and iOS/Android app.

What we collect

When you send a booking request we collect your name, phone number, selected tour and date, number of travellers and any note you write. We use it only to contact you about your booking. We do not require an account.

Saved tours and display settings are stored only on your device.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising or tracking. Data is stored with our hosting/database providers (Vercel, Supabase) solely to run the service.

Retention and deletion

Booking requests are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted. You can ask for deletion any time by calling +976 9003 1771 (or message us on Instagram @elitetravelmn).

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13.
