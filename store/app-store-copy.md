## English listing

### Promotional text

Take the golden line across Mongolia in a 60-second drift score attack.

### Description

**DRIFT KING: STEPPE NEON** is a fast arcade drift challenge set across Ulaanbaatar nights, open Mongolian steppe, and the ancient stone roads of Kharkhorin.

Every 60-second run asks you to:

- Chain long, clean slides without touching the walls.
- Build combo multipliers with angle, speed, and drift time.
- Hit glowing clip rings for bonus score.
- Race the daily condition or choose your own weather.
- Choose between Ulaanbaatar Ring, Open Steppe, and Kharkhorin Pass.
- Play in Mongolian, English, or Simplified Chinese.
- Chase your personal best and climb the online leaderboard.

Designed for landscape play on iPhone and iPad with touch steering, gas, brake, handbrake, and quick reset controls.

**The steppe is wide. Your drift should be wider.**

## Privacy and review notes

The game stores local preferences and personal bests on the device. If the player submits a leaderboard score, the chosen nickname and score-run statistics are sent to the game's leaderboard service. No real-money purchases are required for the core game loop. Confirm the final privacy policy URL and account/data disclosure answers in App Store Connect before submission.
